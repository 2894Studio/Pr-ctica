# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No frameworks, dependencies or build step (user requirement).

## Users

Owners of independent fashion shops: one or two physical stores plus an online shop. Today they keep stock in a spreadsheet or notebook, and the online shop lives in another tool that does not talk to the first. Confirmed by the user. The scene of the stockroom (a customer asks for the green shirt in M, it sold online that morning and nobody noted it) is a validated-later assumption from the brief.

## Product Purpose

Combbi is an inventory app for fashion shops. It shows which garments and which sizes are left, in store and online, without going to the stockroom. Success of this website: owners sign up to the waiting list by email. The product is not open yet; there is no price and there are no customers.

## Positioning

Size comes first. Each garment is seen by size, with what is left in store and online at the same time. Other tools treat size as one more field inside a product; chain-oriented tools add invoicing, accounting and warehouses.

## Operating Context

The owner works on the shop floor with her phone, between customers. The stock number has to be trustworthy at a glance, without counting.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Every sale, in store or online, deducts its size from the same stock (confirmed by the user as the claim the site may make).
- Plain shop vocabulary: garments, sizes, orders. Never "SKU" or "variants".
- Does not do invoicing, accounting or HR. Not for chains or central warehouses.
- Undecided: which online shop platforms it connects to; launch date; price.
- Must not claim: sells more, saves X hours, "real time".

## Brand Commitments

Name Combbi (said "com-bi"). Existing lines: "Gestiona tu tienda de moda, sin líos", "Tu tienda de moda, mejor gestionada", "Moda independiente. Sin complicaciones". Voice: dry and concrete, names what was checked and what was not, no superlatives. Logo and icons from the brand kit (`assets/`), colours and type from `design.md` (binding: bosque, hoja, lima, crema, blanco; Plus Jakarta Sans).

## Evidence on Hand

- Brand kit: wordmark and "C" icon, icons (hanger, box, cart, check, arrow), Plus Jakarta Sans files.
- One generated photo of a shop owner with her phone, cut out: `assets/fotos/duena.webp`. It is an AI-generated image, not a real customer.
- No customers, testimonials, figures, prices or case studies exist. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. The size is the headline: show what is left per size before anything else.
2. One thing per screen; remove work instead of adding it.
3. Say only what can be shown with one screen of the product.
4. Speak like the person who runs the shop floor, not like software.
